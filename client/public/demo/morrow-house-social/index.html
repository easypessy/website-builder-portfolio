Morrow HouseContent direction · 2026

Content that follows the kitchen's week, not a content strategy.

A restaurant posting only finished plates is competing with every other restaurant posting finished plates, usually with worse photographs. Meanwhile the thing that actually makes this kitchen interesting — the Tuesday market run, a chef who knows the farm by name, a board that changes on Wednesday — never appeared anywhere.

So the calendar was built backwards from the rota. Select a day below.

The operational week

The top half of each day is what the kitchen is already doing. The bottom half is the only content that day can realistically produce. Nothing here requires anyone to come in early or stay late.

Caption voice, for people who do not write

The kitchen writes these, not an agency. So the guide is a pair of columns rather than a document — the sous chef can check a caption against it in fifteen seconds.

How we write it

Braised beef shin, tonight only. Twelve portions.

The lamb is from Ashgrove, twenty minutes up the A40. We have used them for four years.

Tuesday market. Everything green today was under £2 a kilo, so expect a lot of it this week.

Two tables left at 8pm. Ring the restaurant, the booking system is slower than we are.

How we do not

Indulge in our mouth-watering braised beef shin — a true culinary journey.

We source only the finest, locally-sourced, artisanal ingredients.

Fresh produce alert! Swipe to see what our amazing team picked up today.

Don't miss out! Limited availability — book now to avoid disappointment.

Designed to be handed over

An agency-run account stops the month the agency stops. This one is built so the staff already on shift can keep it running, which means every format has to survive being shot on a phone in a hurry.

Concept system. It has not been run for a real restaurant, so there are no reach or booking figures here. What is being designed for is survival after handover, not a launch spike.

  1. Phone photography onlyDaylight where possible, kitchen light where not. No studio shoot, ever — it would contradict the whole premise.
  2. One person on rota, not "the team"Whoever opens on Tuesday shoots the market. It is a task on the shift sheet, not a favour.
  3. Templates with the words already in themThe specials-board story has the layout fixed; the only variable is the dish and the number left.
  4. A weekly ten-minute check, not a monthly reviewShort and frequent beats long and quarterly for a business with no marketing person.
