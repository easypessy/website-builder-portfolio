Pulse Method

Educational series · content format · 2026

One mistake.
Shown badly,
then well.

The account looked like every other fitness page: before-and-afters, motivational captions, intimidating physiques. It attracted people who were already fit and put off the beginners the studio actually sells to.

So the whole account became one recurring format. Six slides. A single correction. Nothing aspirational anywhere in it.

Arrow keys work too. Turn subtitles off to see what 70% of the audience would actually get.

Why the format is fixed

Six slides, never five, never eight
A fixed length means the person making it stops deciding how long it should be. That single removed decision is the difference between posting weekly and posting when inspired.
The wrong rep comes first
Recognition before instruction. Someone scrolling has to see themselves in slide two or they will not reach slide four.
Every frame carries its own caption
It is designed to be watched silently on a commute. If a slide only works with audio, it does not ship.
The last slide is not a sales pitch
It is one thing to try in the next session. The booking link lives in the profile, where it does not cost anything to ignore.
Not allowed in this account
  • Before-and-after photographs
  • The words "journey", "transform", "grind" or "beast"
  • Weight or body-fat numbers of any kind
  • Stock gym photography — everything is shot here, on the actual equipment
