    Four states and the transitions between them

    The design work in a booking system is not the booking form. It is deciding what happens at each transition, and which ones are allowed to happen without a person.

    Booked

    Slot held against live availability. Double-booking is prevented at the write, not checked afterwards.

    → confirmation sent instantly

    Confirmed

    Client has replied or tapped confirm. Reminders are scheduled at 24 hours and 2 hours.

    → reminders queued

    Cancelled

    Released immediately rather than held. The studio would rather have an open slot it can sell than a courtesy hold.

    → waitlist offer fires

    Refilled

    First waitlist client to accept takes it. Later acceptances get a straight answer, not silence.

    → others told it has gone

    What the client actually receives

    Three messages, written to be read on a phone between other things. No marketing attached to a reminder.

    Confirmation

    Hi Amara — you're booked in for a cut and finish on Tuesday 14 October at 10:30 with Rose. About 75 minutes.

    Reply CHANGE if you need a different time.

    Sent immediately on booking
    Reminder

    Tomorrow at 10:30 with Rose. Parking is easiest on Mill Street after 10.

    If you can't make it, tell us now and we'll offer the slot to someone on the waitlist.

    24 hours before · second at 2 hours
    Waitlist offer

    A slot has opened: Tuesday 14 October, 10:30 with Rose.

    First to reply YES takes it. We'll tell you either way within the hour.

    Sent the moment a cancellation lands

    Honest limits

    Concept workflow. It has not run in a live studio, so there is no measured no-show reduction and no recovered revenue figure to quote.

    • The week above is scripted. Cancelling and refilling are demonstrations, not a connected calendar.
    • Waitlist refill only works if the waitlist is real. A studio with three names on it will see very little.
    • A same-day cancellation at 9pm is unlikely to refill whatever the system does. The mechanism helps most with 24–72 hours of notice.
    • Messaging costs money per send. At high volume that has to be priced in before any saving is claimed.

    If cancellations are costing you the most, start there.
