Sign-ups were fine. Attendance was the problem.

The dashboard looked healthy. Trial bookings were coming in at a reasonable cost, the ads were working, and the studio was quietly losing money every week.

A no-show trial is worse than a lead that never converts. The coach's hour is already committed, the slot cannot be resold at short notice, and nobody is standing in it. The cost is not the wasted marketing spend — it is the wasted capacity behind it.

So the work moved off acquisition entirely and onto the four days between booking a trial and turning up to it.

The four days in between

Four messages. None of them sell anything, because the sale already happened — the person has agreed to come. Everything here exists to reduce the two reasons people quietly drop out: forgetting, and dreading it.

And when they still do not turn up

What most places send

We missed you at your session today! Our coaches had prepared specially for you. Late cancellations affect our ability to serve other members. Rebook here: [link]

Guilt, a mild accusation, and a link. It reads as a telling-off, and the most common reason for a no-show — nerves — is exactly the thing this makes worse.

What is sent instead

Hi Sam — Dele here. You were down for 7pm, no problem at all. For what it is worth, about a third of first sessions get moved. It is usually work, and occasionally it is nerves, and both are fine. Thursday 7pm and Saturday 9am are open if you want either. If now is not the moment, that is also a completely reasonable answer and I will leave you be.

Named person, no guilt, the awkward reason acknowledged out loud, two concrete times, and an explicit permission to stop. One message. There is no second chase.
