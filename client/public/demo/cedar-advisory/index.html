How an engagement actually runs

Written out because the most common reason a small firm loses a pitch is that the buyer cannot picture what the next eight weeks will feel like.

01

Forty-five minutes

A conversation, not a pitch. We will tell you in that call whether we think this is a Cedar problem. Roughly a third of the time it is not, and we will say who we would ring instead.

No charge
02

A two-page scope

What we will do, what we will not, who does it, what it costs and when it finishes. Two pages, not twenty. If it needs twenty pages the scope is not clear enough yet.

Within 5 days
03

The work

Done by the named person. Weekly thirty-minute call, same slot every week, cancelled by us if there is nothing to say. You get the working documents as they exist, not a polished reveal at the end.

4–12 weeks
04

Handover, then out

A written recommendation and a session with whoever has to carry it. We do not stay on retainer afterwards as a matter of course — if the answer was any good, you should not need us next quarter.

Final 2 weeks
05

Six months later

One call, unbilled, to find out what actually happened. It is the only way we learn whether the advice was right, and it is frequently uncomfortable.

Work we decline

Published because it is the fastest way for a prospect to rule us out, and because every item here is something we took on once and did badly.

Anything where the decision is already made

If the recommendation is required to be a particular recommendation, you need a different kind of firm. We have done this once and it ended badly for everyone four months later.

Brand, marketing and design

Not our discipline. We will recommend two people and take no fee for doing it.

Engagements under four weeks

The set-up cost is the same as a twelve-week piece and the output is thin. Below that, a long conversation is usually better value and we will just have the conversation.

Interim management

Sitting in the chair and advising on the chair are different jobs and the second one stops working the moment you do the first.

Panels and frameworks

We are four people. The administrative cost of being on a panel exceeds the work it produces.

Anything requiring more than six concurrent engagements

Six is the limit. When we are full we say so and give you a date, rather than taking it and subcontracting it.
