Will this actually work for me?

Sometimes it doesn't. Roughly a third of people feel clearly better, a third notice something smaller they'd still call worthwhile, and a third don't get much from it — sometimes because it's the wrong approach, sometimes because it's the wrong time. We will tell you at session four which of those we think is happening.

Do I have to have something serious to come?

No. Most people who contact us are managing. Work is fine, mostly. They want an hour a week that belongs to them, with someone who isn't a friend or a spouse.

What if I cry, or can't speak at all?

Both happen weekly. Silence is allowed to fill a whole session and the hour still counts for something. There are tissues on the table and nobody will comment on you using them.

Will you tell me what to do?

Rarely, and never about big decisions like leaving a job or a marriage. If you want direct advice, say so and we'll be clear about where we will and won't give it.

What stays private — and what doesn't?

Everything stays in the room, with three legal exceptions: a serious risk to your life, a risk to a child or vulnerable adult, and a court order. If one of those happens we will tell you what we're doing and who we're telling, unless telling you would increase the risk. We do not discuss clients with each other by name.

Is anything written down?

Brief notes after each session — themes, not transcripts. You can ask to read them at any time. They're kept encrypted for seven years, then deleted.

What does it cost, and what if I can't afford it?

₦25,000 an hour. Four reduced-fee places each week at ₦10,000, decided by a two-line form and not by an interview about your finances. Ask. It doesn't affect how you're treated.

What if I don't like you?

Say so, or just don't book again — no cancellation fee, no follow-up email asking why. If you'd like a name elsewhere we'll give you three, including people who work differently to us.

How many sessions will I need?

We don't know at the start and anyone who tells you a number at the start is guessing. Most people come between six and twenty times. Some come twice.

Can I bring someone with me?

To the building, yes. Into the room, only for a first session, and only if you'd otherwise not come.

Will you tell my GP or my employer?

Only if you ask us to in writing. Employer-funded sessions are the one place this gets complicated — if your employer is paying, they receive dates attended and nothing else, and we'll show you exactly what that report looks like before you agree to it.

When should I go somewhere else instead?

If you are thinking about ending your life, please contact your GP, go to A&E, or call a crisis line now — we are not a crisis service and cannot answer out of hours. If your main difficulty is alcohol or drugs, a specialist service will help you more than we will, and we'll help you find one.

What we won't say

No testimonials on this site. Quoting a former client's recovery, even anonymously, turns their hour into our marketing. No success rates either — the honest answer is on the first question above.
