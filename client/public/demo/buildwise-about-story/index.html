The job that overran

Plot 9, Yaba — eleven weeks late

We found an unmarked drain run under the footprint of the extension in week three. We should have found it at survey; we didn't dig a trial hole because the drawings looked clear, and that was our call to make.

Redesigning the foundation cost the client ₦2.1m in extra concrete and eleven weeks. We absorbed the redesign fee and the standing time for our own crew. We did not absorb the concrete, and we told them that on the day rather than at the end.

Since then we dig a trial hole on every job, which adds about ₦180,000 and half a day. It's on every quote as a line item. Now you know why.

What we say no to

Loft conversions, listed buildings, and anything where the client has already fallen out with a previous builder and wants us to finish it cheaply. The last one always ends badly for everybody.

The guarantee, in plain words

  • Structural work: ten years, insurance-backed, certificate issued at handover.
  • Everything else we install: two years, parts and labour.
  • If something fails in those periods we come back and fix it. You don't pay a call-out and you don't argue with a call centre.
  • What it doesn't cover: wear, movement in materials you specified against our advice, and work another trade has since altered.
