The five emailsRules

Five emails for people who downloaded the syllabus and went quiet.

They asked for something, got it, and vanished — which usually means they're interested but not convinced. The sequence assumes that, rather than assuming they forgot.

The rules behind the sequence

Teach in email two

A real lesson, useful even if they never buy. It's the only way to earn email three.

Say the price early

Email three, not email five. Hiding it just delays the same decision.

Make "no" easy

Email five offers a graceful exit. People who leave politely come back later; people who feel trapped don't.

Sample sequence written for demonstration. No send data, so no open or conversion rates are claimed.
