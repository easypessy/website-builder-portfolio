The three messages after the first conversation that nobody ever sends.

Most small firms lose work in the gap between "I'll send something over" and nothing. Not because the lead was cold — because Tuesday happened. This is the smallest workflow that closes that gap without becoming nagging.

The rules that keep it from being spam

Any reply kills the sequence

Including "not right now". The workflow's job is to start a conversation, not to finish a funnel.

Each message carries something

A document, an answer, a decision. No message exists purely to check in.

Three, then silence

The fourth message is where goodwill goes to die. There is no fourth message.

Day 14 ends it properly: "I'll assume the timing isn't right and stop emailing. If it changes, reply to this and we'll pick it up where we left off." Nobody has to feel rude, and the door stays open.
