Studio Common

Founder content system · concept · 2026

Four hours a month is the actual constraint. So it is the brief.

The founder's expertise was real and completely undocumented. Every content plan she had been given assumed a weekly writing habit she was never going to have, so nothing was ever published — which looked like a discipline problem and was really a design problem.

She talks for forty-five minutes once a month. The system does the rest. No writing from scratch, no design dependency, no video editing. The output is four text posts, which is also why the whole thing is typographic — over-designed thought leadership reads as ghostwritten.

The prompt sheet

Printed, on the desk, same five prompts every month. The consistency is the point — she is not being interviewed, she is answering the same questions about a different month.

Monthly recording · 45 minutes

01
What did a client get wrong this month that you have now seen twenty times?
02
What did you change your mind about?
03
What did you refuse to do, and why?
04
What is the thing everyone in this industry says that you think is wrong?
05
What would you tell someone starting the job you do, in their first week?
Three rules for the recording. Talk as if to one person, not an audience. Name the specific case before the general point. If a sentence needs a diagram, cut it — this format has no diagrams.

Quarterly themes, so it adds up to an argument

Twelve unconnected opinions is a feed. Twelve posts circling one question is a position, and a position is the thing that eventually gets someone to email.

Concept system. It has not been run for a real founder, so there are no impressions, follower or enquiry figures here, and the transcript above is written rather than recorded. What is being designed is a habit that survives a busy month — the only measure that matters before there is any data at all.
