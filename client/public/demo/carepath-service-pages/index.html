Your first physiotherapy appointment

What happens, how long it takes, what it costs, and what this appointment will not do.

Reading age: 12Clinically checked 14 Mar 2026Read time: 3 minutesAlso available: large print, Yoruba

What happens, step by step

  1. You talk, for about ten minutesThe physiotherapist asks when the pain started, what makes it worse, and what you have already tried. Bring the names of any medicines you take.
  2. You move, for about fifteen minutesYou will be asked to bend, walk, and lift your arms or legs. You can stop at any point. You may be asked to remove outer clothing — you can ask for someone else to be in the room.
  3. You get an explanationIn plain words: what the physiotherapist thinks is happening, and how sure they are. If they are not sure, they will say so.
  4. You leave with a planUsually three or four exercises on a printed sheet, and a date for the next visit if you need one.

What this appointment does not cover

  • It is not a scan. If you need an X-ray or MRI, you will be referred and that costs extra.
  • It is not a prescription. Physiotherapists here cannot prescribe medication.
  • It is not a sick note. Ask your GP for that.
  • It will not fix the problem in one visit. Most people need three to six.

When to go somewhere else instead

Go to A&E, not to us, if you have numbness in both legs, loss of bladder or bowel control, or a fever with back pain. These are rare, but they need a hospital the same day.
