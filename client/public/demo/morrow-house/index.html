Tonight's menu

Six dishes. Changed Wednesday.

Everything is cooked to order by two people. If something runs out it is marked here within a few minutes, which is why there is no printed menu to be out of date.

Hours

——
Kitchen closes thirty minutes before the room does. Last orders are taken at the bar, not by phone.

Thirty covers, two cooks, one delivery a week.

We buy on Tuesday and write the menu on Wednesday morning, which is the only sensible order to do it in. Six dishes is what two people can cook properly in a service.

There is no signature dish. There is usually a chicken, because it sells and it is good, and the other five change with what came back from the market.

We do not do tasting menus, we do not take a card to hold a booking, and the kitchen will happily cook something plain for a child without making a performance of it.

What happens on which day

Regulars mostly want to know two things: has the menu changed, and is it busy. Both are on this grid rather than in a paragraph.

◆

Closed Sunday 14th for a private party

The room is taken all evening. We are open as normal on the Tuesday after. Anyone with a booking that night has been rung — if you think you have one and nobody has called, please ring us.

Booking

Tables of one to six can be booked here. Seven or more, ring us — it needs the long table and a conversation about the menu.

We hold tables for fifteen minutes. If you are running late, a message is genuinely fine and we will keep it.

Walk-ins get the four bar seats, which are kept back every night and are not bookable.

Before you ask

Dietary requirements. Tell us when you book, not on the night. With six dishes and two cooks, notice is the difference between a good plate and an apology.

Children. Welcome, always. There is no children's menu; we will cook something plain.

Dogs. Yes, in the front half of the room.

Service charge. Not added. Tips are split between everyone working that night.
