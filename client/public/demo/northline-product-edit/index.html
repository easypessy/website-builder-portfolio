The three moves, and the rules around them

  • Made of — material, weight, origin, construction. Numbers wherever a number exists.
  • Sits like — the fit in the body, including where it runs large or small. Say the unflattering thing.
  • Wear it when — the honest occasion. If it's a two-months-a-year coat, say so.
  • No "elevate", "effortless", "must-have", "curated", "investment piece".
  • No superlatives the customer can't verify by touching it.
  • If a fact isn't known, it's left out rather than softened into adjective.
