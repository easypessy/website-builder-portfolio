Before adding channels, make the one that already works deliberate.

A short strategy document, not a deck. Four sections, one recommendation, and the rejected options written down so they do not have to be argued about again in March.

01The finding

All work arrives by referral. That was presented as the problem. It is not the problem — it is the only thing working, and it has never been given a process.

Every project in two years came from a personal recommendationThe offer is good and the market trusts it. Demand generation is not the missing piece.Strength

03Making referral deliberate

The mechanism is unglamorous: a list of past clients, a reason to make contact that is not a request, and a fixed date in the diary. The prompt below is the template — fill it in and it writes the message she would actually send.

Referral prompt

Never "do you know anyone who needs me". Always a specific, useful thing sent to a specific person, with the ask second and small.

Four dates a year, in the calendar, with the list attached. That is the entire referral system. It replaces remembering.

04The publishing rhythm

Two channels, sized to the time that actually exists. Nothing here assumes a week she does not have, which is the reason the previous five attempts stopped.

Week 1

Write one piece

Nine hundred words on a question a client asked in the last month. No editorial calendar — the calendar is the client.

90 min
Week 2

Publish and send

Posted, then emailed to the list. Same words both places. No rewriting for format.

30 min
Week 3

Two replies

Answer whoever responded, properly. This is where the work comes from, not the post itself.

45 min
Week 4

Pipeline, ten minutes

Update the spreadsheet. Who is where, what the next contact is, when.

15 min

Three hours a month, leaving an hour of the budget unspent. And it is a spreadsheet, not a CRM — a sole consultant with twelve live conversations does not have a data problem, and the licence fee would buy nothing she cannot do with four columns.

Concept strategy for an invented consultancy. No revenue, enquiry or conversion figures appear here and none are implied — the scores are a structured judgement, not a measurement. The value on offer is the order of operations: systematise the working channel before funding a new one.
