How it stays honest

No answer without a row

If the catalogue has no matching SKU, the assistant says it doesn't stock it rather than describing something plausible.

Stock counts, not "available"

Two left reads as two left. Vague availability language is what causes wasted journeys.

Reserve, don't promise

It can hold an item for 24 hours against the real stock record. It can't promise a restock date it doesn't have.
